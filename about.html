About Me

After over a decade of working in professional kitchens, Joseph decided to step away from his career as a chef and pursue a long-standing interest in photography. He is now beginning his formal education in photography in New York City, bringing with him the discipline, attention to detail, and curiosity that shaped his years in the kitchen. His work is still evolving, but is always drawn to images that explore place, objects, and the stories they carry.
